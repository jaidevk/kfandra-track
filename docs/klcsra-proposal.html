What it is, at a glance

Overview
The problem today

Match details live in people's heads and scattered messages. Turning them into website updates, MMG points and balance-sheet numbers means re-typing everything by hand — and tallying every stat manually.

What KLCSRA does

One admin records the match once — sport, clubs, squads and every player's stats. The app totals each player's KR & MMG, produces a report to copy out, keeps season tables, feeds the balance sheets, then locks on Submit.

What gets captured per match
Sport

Football, Rugby, or a Variation. Drives which stats appear.

Date & duration

Match date; duration typed in minutes.

Two clubs + venue role

Each side a club, tagged Home / Away / Neutral.

Score per team

Final score entered as a number per side.

Up to 6 players / side

Picked as "J. Karanth". Empty slots blank. Grows to 11 + subs later.

Per-player stats

A tap-to-add popup of 13 stats, each paying KR + MMG.

Three things it produces
  • Copy-ready match report for the website.
  • Season tables — leaderboards + standings (auto).
  • Per-player KR & MMG totals — KR fills the balance sheet; MMG shown to copy into MMG.
Fixed in-app · players do nothing

Every payout, result and standing is computed by the app from what's recorded. Managers/players don't re-enter anything; once you Submit, the match locks (only coach+ can reopen).

Access: KLCSRA sits inside the existing Admin area (staff only), next to the Club Balance Sheet review. Managers and players never see it — though an admin can hand a Manager the device to fill the live match; they still can't edit a submitted one.

How an admin fills it in
1
Date, sport & duration

Pick date, tap the sport, type the minutes.

2
The two clubs + role

A club per side, each tagged Home / Away / Neutral.

3
Score per side

Enter each team's final score (a number — independent of the tallies, so rugby works).

4
Squads — up to 6 each

Fill slots from the member list. Leave unused slots empty.

5
Each player's stats

Tap Stats → the popup (next page). The button shows a live tally, e.g. G1 · Ass1.

6
Submit & lock

Finalises the match, computes all KR/MMG, and makes it read-only. Only coach / super-admin can reopen.

Autosave, then Submit. Every edit saves as you go (offline-safe) so nothing's lost mid-match. Submit is the deliberate lock — a helper Manager can fill the live match but can't touch a submitted one.

League standings points

By match size (total players in the match):

  • 6 or more players: Win 3 · Draw 1 · Loss 0
  • Fewer than 6: Win 0.2 · Draw 0.05 · Loss 0

Margin bonus: win by ≥20 → +1 to the winner, −1 to the loser.

Combined match (two halves)

Two sides carry across the halves; each half is a normal 1-v-1. Half-win = 0.2; aggregate-score winner = +0.1 to both its clubs.

Example: KL+BOCI vs DP+SOG — H1 KL beat DP, H2 SOG beat BOCI, KL+BOCI won on aggregate:
