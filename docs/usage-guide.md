# The Jacaranda App — Player Guide

A quick guide to logging your sessions, gym work, and diet. Everything you
enter **saves automatically** — there's no "submit" button to remember.

Open the app at **https://kfandra-track.vercel.app** on your phone. Tip: use
your browser's *Add to Home Screen* so it opens like a normal app.

---

## 1. Getting in

### First time — Register
1. Tap **Create an account**.
2. Enter your **name** (your player nickname), your **phone number**
   (10-digit mobile), and choose a **4-digit PIN**.
3. Tap **Create account**. You're in straight away — no approval needed.

### Coming back — Sign in
Enter your **phone number** and **PIN**, tap **Sign in**. You stay logged in
for about two months, so you won't have to do this often.

> Forgot your PIN? There's no self-reset yet — message KFANDRA/admin and
> they'll sort it out.

---

## 2. The home screen

You'll see three things to track. Tap any card to open it:

- ⚽ **MMG** — points for a training session.
- 🏋️ **Gym** — your gym work for a day.
- 🥗 **Diet** — what you ate today.

---

## 3. MMG — session points

Pick the **session date** at the top (Tue/Thu/Sat dates are listed). Your
running points total shows large at the top and updates live as you fill things
in. There are four parts:

### 1) Participation (once per session)
- **Confirmation order** and **Arrival order** — your place in line. These feed
  the order-of-arrival points (worked out once everyone's in).
- Toggles for packing/unpacking help and confirming availability — tap the ones
  that apply. Each shows the points it's worth.

### 2) Performance — one card per game type
This is where you log the games you played. Tap **+ Add a game**, then in the
sheet:
- Pick the **game type** (Football short, Rugby, Fooba, etc.).
- Under **Result · how many games**, use the **+ / −** steppers to enter how
  many of that type you **Won**, **Drew**, and **Lost**.
- Below that, tap up the **stats** you racked up across those games — goals,
  assists, saves, tackles, and so on. Penalties (cards, fouls) are at the bottom
  and subtract points.
- Tap **Done**.

The card then shows a summary like **"Football short · 2W · 1D"** and the points
it earned. Add a separate card for each *type* of game you played — they all add
into your session total. Everything is cumulative: 3 wins = 3× the win points,
5 goals = 5× the goal points, etc.

### 3) Other (free-form)
Anything not covered above — add a description and a points value (can be
negative). E.g. a skill you used to entertain — "Shibobho +300" or
"Ardiles flick +300".

### 4) Narration (optional)
A few words on how the session went.

### Finalize
Tap **Finalize session** when you're done. This shows the provisional
order-of-arrival points. **It is not an approval** — you can still edit
afterwards, and it'll keep saving.

---

## 4. Gym — daily logging

Open **Gym**, pick the day, and log your work: choose the **body part /
exercise** and record your **sets and reps**. It saves as you go. Gym logging is
per day and isn't scored — it's your training record.

---

## 5. Diet — what you ate

Open **Diet** to see the day's meals laid out by time of day. **Pick the date**
at the top (it opens on today, and you can go back to log an earlier day you
missed). For each meal, **tap the foods** you ate to log them; tap again / use
the steppers to set how many. You can add a custom item if something isn't on
the list. Diet saves automatically — no scoring, just a record.

---

## 6. Good to know

- **It saves itself.** You'll see "Saving…" then "Saved ✓". If you're offline it
  saves on your phone and syncs when you're back — you won't lose anything.
- **You can always edit.** Nothing locks. Come back and adjust your entries any
  time.
- **One account per phone.** Your phone number is your login.

Questions or something looks wrong? Tell KFANDRA/admin.
